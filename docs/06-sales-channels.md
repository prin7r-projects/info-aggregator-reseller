# 06 — Sales channels

## Channel mix (year 1)

| Channel | Mix | Why this fits Annotedly |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn long-form posts | 30% | Mireille and Ravi both consume long-form on LinkedIn. Annotedly&rsquo;s editorial voice translates well to a 1,000-word weekly post about &ldquo;what we deduplicated this week.&rdquo; |
| Direct outreach (curated list) | 25% | A list of 200 boutique consultancies and 80 vertical-SaaS partnerships leads. Three-touch cadence: warm intro → sample dossier → 20-min call. Hand-written, not sequenced. |
| Reseller-amplified distribution | 20% | Every reseller-license deal gives Annotedly implicit reach to the reseller&rsquo;s top accounts. The Annotedly-credit footnote on each issue acts as a soft ad. |
| Methodology essays on Substack | 10% | Quarterly long-form on sourcing, dedupe, leak-provenance verification. Cross-posted from the Annotedly site. Earns mentions from analyst-of-analysts publications. |
| Conference + meetup presence | 8% | EU regulator-conference talks (Brussels, Frankfurt) and quarterly fintech analyst meetups. Speaking, not sponsoring. |
| SEO long-tail | 5% | Pages titled &ldquo;What changed in [vertical] this week&rdquo; — captures Mireille&rsquo;s Tuesday-morning search intent. |
| Paid ads | 2% | Tightly targeted LinkedIn ads to job-titles only (analyst, partnerships lead at vertical-SaaS). Capped at $1,500/mo Year 1. |

## What we explicitly do NOT do

- **Cold email blasts.** Burns the brand. Annotedly is sold one trust-relationship at a time.
- **Affiliate networks.** Misaligned incentives — affiliates pump volume on the wrong audience.
- **Ad networks (Google Ads, programmatic).** Audience is too small to be worth the spend; intent signal is too weak.
- **Influencer sponsorships on YouTube / TikTok / Instagram.** Wrong format, wrong audience.
- **&ldquo;Free trial&rdquo; entry on the public page.** A free tier would attract the anti-personas described in `05-audience-profile.md`. Sample-on-request via email is the wedge.
- **Webinars produced by Annotedly.** Webinars feel like marketing; the brand is editorial. We will *appear* on other people&rsquo;s panels, not host our own.

## Channel-specific cadence

### LinkedIn (Mireille channel)
- 1 long-form post every two weeks (Mon 09:00 ET).
- Topic mix: 50% &ldquo;what we deduplicated this week&rdquo;, 30% methodology, 20% &ldquo;a leaked memo we declined to publish (and why)&rdquo;.
- Comments are answered, not ignored — but the editorial desk does the answering, not a community manager.

### Direct outreach (Ravi channel)
- 30 named accounts per month, three-touch cadence over 6 weeks.
- Touch 1: warm intro from a peer (use Mireille-tier subscribers as advocates) or a personalized note referencing their last newsletter.
- Touch 2: sample co-branded dossier, rendered with their actual logo as a placeholder.
- Touch 3: 20-min call.
- Conversion target: 6% to subscribe at Bundle, 1% to Reseller in year 1.

### Reseller amplification
- Each Reseller-license issue ships with a small &ldquo;Annotedly An-SRC-26&rdquo; line in the footer (subtle, not prominent). Resellers can opt-out for an additional fee.
- Partnership pages on the Annotedly site name each reseller (with permission). This is anti-stealth — being seen as the editorial backbone behind named firms is the marketing.
