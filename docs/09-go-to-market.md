# 09 — Go-to-market plan

A 90-day plan covering launch through first six paying subscribers and first reseller-license deal.

## Pre-launch (week 0)

- [ ] Wave 2 landing live at `info-aggregator-reseller.prin7r.com` with NOWPayments crypto checkout (this is the Wave 2 deliverable).
- [ ] Sample of the prior-week dossier prepared (fintech vertical) — PDF + email-template version.
- [ ] Source list for fintech vertical drafted (40-60 feeds).
- [ ] Direct-outreach target list assembled: 30 boutique consultancies + 10 vertical-SaaS partnerships leads = 40 named accounts.
- [ ] Methodology essay drafted: *&ldquo;Why every paragraph in Annotedly ships with a source-ID.&rdquo;*
- [ ] LinkedIn editorial-desk profile updated.

## Week 1 — public launch

- Mon: methodology essay published on Substack and the Annotedly site.
- Tue: LinkedIn long-form post: *&ldquo;What we deduplicated this week — fintech edition.&rdquo;*
- Wed: first wave of 10 direct-outreach emails to the named-account list. Hand-written, references the recipient&rsquo;s last published note.
- Thu: editorial desk monitors inbox for sample-dossier requests.
- Fri 09:00 ET: first issue ships. Dedupe ratio published.

**Target.** 4 sample requests, 1 paid subscriber by EoW.

## Week 2 — methodology depth

- Mon: second methodology essay: *&ldquo;Three leaks we declined this month.&rdquo;*
- Tue: second batch of 10 direct-outreach emails.
- Wed: 1:1 calls with the 4 sample-request recipients.
- Fri: second issue. First subscriber gets it via the live feed.

**Target.** 7 cumulative sample requests, 2 paid subscribers.

## Week 3 — reseller seed

- Mon: third LinkedIn long-form: *&ldquo;What a Annotedly footnote ledger looks like.&rdquo;*
- Tue: third batch of 10 outreach. Includes 5 vertical-SaaS partnerships leads (Ravi-tier).
- Thu: first reseller call (20 min). If positive, send sample co-branded dossier.
- Fri: third issue.

**Target.** 4 paid subscribers, 1 reseller call complete.

## Weeks 4-6 — biotech vertical preview

- Source list for biotech vertical published.
- &ldquo;Coming next: biotech grants&rdquo; LinkedIn post.
- First Bundle subscriber expected (Week 5-6) — a consultancy that wants both fintech and biotech.

**Target.** 6 paid subscribers (5 Single, 1 Bundle), 1 reseller call deeper, 18 cumulative sample requests.

## Weeks 7-9 — first reseller license

- First reseller-license deal closes (target: $4,800 setup paid via NOWPayments, USDT on Tron).
- Reseller onboarding: collect wordmark, accent, logo, footer line, target URL.
- First co-branded issue ships in Week 9.

**Target.** 7 paid subscribers, 1 reseller live.

## Weeks 10-12 — depth

- Second methodology essay sequence: leak verification, dedupe deep-dive.
- Second reseller call (sourced via the first reseller&rsquo;s peer network).
- Govtech procurement vertical alpha drafts circulated to 3 paid subscribers for feedback.

**Target.** 9 paid subscribers, 1 reseller live, 2 reseller calls in flight.

## Day-90 retrospective checklist

- [ ] Were dedupe ratios published every week? (Yes / No → why)
- [ ] Did sample-to-subscribe conversion hit 15%? (Year 1 plan target: 18%)
- [ ] Did the methodology essay sequence earn at least 1 backlink from an analyst-of-analysts publication?
- [ ] How many direct-outreach emails landed vs. bounced? (Target: <5% bounce)
- [ ] Reseller pipeline at end of Day 90: 1 live + 2 in flight.
- [ ] Editorial desk health: any week missed a Friday ship? Any retraction? (Target: 0/0.)

## Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Mireille thinks $499 is too much | Sample dossier is good enough that 18% subscribe; price is anchored against junior-analyst hours saved. |
| Reseller asks for a custom feature outside white-label scope | Reseller-license scope is documented (`docs/07-sales-strategy.md`). Edge requests go to the Wave-3 product roadmap. |
| Editorial desk burnout (single-person op risk) | Friday-only shipping cadence + Wed editorial cutoff + Mon-Tue ingest automation. Hire a second analyst at month 6. |
| A leak provenance turns out to be wrong post-publication | Public retraction next issue + named editor accountability + methodology page documents process. |
| NOWPayments invoice fails for a buyer | The pricing UI surfaces the error and a `mailto:` fallback. Net-30 wire is offered for Reseller-tier without a question. |
