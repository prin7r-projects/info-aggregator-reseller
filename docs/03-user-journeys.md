# 03 — User journeys

Three primary journeys, each grounded in real source material a Wave-2 reader could plausibly have on her desk this week.

## Journey 1 — Discovery → first value (consultancy analyst)

**Persona.** Mireille, fintech analyst, Brussels.

1. **Trigger.** Mireille gets a forwarded LinkedIn post from a peer pointing at a prior-week Bureau dossier on the FDIC brokered-deposit rule.
2. **Land.** She opens `info-aggregator-reseller.prin7r.com` on Tuesday morning. The hero shows an actual dossier excerpt — the same paragraph her peer forwarded, with footnote markers and source-IDs visible.
3. **Sample request.** She clicks `Sample digest →` and emails the listed address with her firm name and the vertical (fintech). Bureau replies within four working hours with the prior-week PDF and a direct link to the JSON feed sample.
4. **First value.** Mireille reads the prior-week dossier on her commute. She notices the dedupe ratio (17:1) and the way each claim links to a `§ FED-25-1144`-style ID. She forwards two paragraphs to her partner with a note: *&ldquo;This is the format I&rsquo;ve been trying to build manually for six months.&rdquo;*
5. **Subscribe.** Wednesday she comes back to the page, picks **Single — fintech**, clicks the NOWPayments CTA. USDC on Polygon. Invoice paid in 4 minutes.
6. **First issue.** Friday 09:00 ET (15:00 CET) she gets the issue. The body matches what she saw at sample. By 11:00 CET she has shipped her client note. Time saved this week: 3 hours of Thursday-night Federal Register reading.

## Journey 2 — Recurring use (analyst, weeks 2-12)

1. **Habit.** Friday 15:00 CET dossier arrives. Mireille spends 18 minutes reading it instead of 3 hours crawling. She copies out 4-6 paragraphs into her own client note, citing Bureau by source-ID where appropriate.
2. **Feed integration.** Week 4: she connects the JSON feed to a private Notion database. Each issue becomes structured rows her firm can search later (six months in, she has a 700-row archive).
3. **Cross-vertical interest.** Week 7: a partner asks for the climate-tech disclosures dossier for a new client. Mireille upgrades to **Bundle** in two clicks.
4. **Renewal moment.** Month 3: the firm reviews subscriptions. Bureau is the one Mireille is still using. She defends the renewal with a screenshot of her Notion archive and a bullet on time saved.
5. **Reseller seed.** Month 4: the firm partner asks if Bureau will let them resell under the firm masthead. Mireille forwards them to the Reseller-license page.

## Journey 3 — Reseller (partnerships lead)

**Persona.** Ravi, partnerships lead, $30M ARR vertical SaaS.

1. **Trigger.** Ravi&rsquo;s last newsletter format died. He&rsquo;s scouting for an editorial product he can syndicate without writing.
2. **Discovery.** A peer at another vendor mentions Bureau. Ravi opens the page, scrolls to **Reseller mockup** — sees Bureau house masthead vs. *Atelier Northrop* masthead side by side.
3. **Diligence.** Ravi reads the methodology pull-quote and the FAQ row on white-label scope. He emails `hello@info-aggregator-reseller.prin7r.com` with his firm name and three vertical interests (govtech, climate-tech, semis).
4. **Sample co-brand.** Bureau replies in 24h with a prior-week dossier rendered under a placeholder *(your firm)* masthead. Ravi shares it internally. The CMO and CEO both react with &ldquo;this looks like an editorial product, not a comms piece.&rdquo;
5. **License open.** Ravi clicks the Reseller plan&rsquo;s NOWPayments CTA. $4,800 setup fee. Invoice paid via USDT on Tron in 3 minutes.
6. **Onboarding.** Bureau collects the wordmark, accent hex (matching Ravi&rsquo;s product brand), logo SVG, footer line, and the URL where the dossier should live (`research.<their-domain>.com`). 5 working days later the first co-branded issue ships to Ravi&rsquo;s top-80 list.
7. **Recurring value.** Month 2: open rate on the new newsletter is 42% vs. 18% on Ravi&rsquo;s old format. Month 3: he adds a fourth vertical for $1,200/mo more.

## Anti-journey (we want to fail this fast)

A solo content marketer searching &ldquo;cheap AI newsletter tool&rdquo; lands on the page. She sees crypto-only checkout, $499/mo entry, a methodology section about footnote ledgers, and zero free tier. She bounces. **This is the desired outcome.** Bureau is not for her, and serving her well would cost the brand.
