# 07 — Sales strategy

## Motion

**Hybrid: PLG for Single + Bundle, sales-led for Reseller-license.**

- **Single ($499 / vertical / month):** self-serve. NOWPayments hosted invoice. The buyer is Mireille, paying on a personal card or expensing same-week. No sales call required. Sample-on-request is the only human touch before the credit card.
- **Bundle ($1,499 / month, all five verticals):** mostly self-serve. A short &ldquo;ICP fit&rdquo; email is sent within 24h of subscription confirming what the firm specializes in (so the editorial desk can prioritize relevant cross-vertical anchors).
- **Reseller-license ($4,800 setup + $1,200 / added vertical / month):** sales-led. Always a 20-30 minute call before invoice. Goal of the call: confirm the reseller&rsquo;s distribution list size, decide on accent + masthead, set the launch issue date, agree on the footer-line credit.

## Pricing tiers (full)

| Tier | Price | What&rsquo;s included | Who buys |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single | $499 / vertical / mo | One vertical. Friday dossier (PDF + email), JSON + CSV feed, footnote ledger, source list, methodology page. | Mireille and her boutique-consultancy peers. |
| Bundle | $1,499 / mo (all five) | Fintech · Biotech · Govtech · Climate · Semis. Quarterly editorial review call. Cross-vertical index every 4 issues. | Mid-size consultancies (12-60 staff) covering more than one vertical. |
| Reseller | $4,800 setup + $1,200 / added vertical / mo | Up to 3 verticals at launch. Re-mastheaded issues. Custom URL, email-from, footer line. Methodology page co-branded with Bureau. | Vertical-SaaS partnership teams, syndicators, mid-market consultancies with 500+ distribution lists. |

Add-ons (post-launch, Wave 3):
- **Custom dossier on demand.** $4,800 per dossier. Bureau&rsquo;s editorial desk produces a one-off issue on a topic outside our standing verticals (e.g. &ldquo;US-China semiconductor export controls deep dive&rdquo; for a private-equity client). 6-week delivery.
- **Reseller branded methodology page.** $1,200 one-time. Bureau drafts a methodology page in the reseller&rsquo;s voice that points to the Bureau back-end without naming it.

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| &ldquo;$499/mo is more than Bloomberg.&rdquo; | A Bloomberg seat is $25k+/yr. Bureau is $6k. The comparable is a junior analyst spending Thursday-night on the Federal Register. |
| &ldquo;Can we get a free trial?&rdquo; | Free sample of last week&rsquo;s issue, on request. No free month — protects the brand and the audience. |
| &ldquo;Why crypto-only?&rdquo; | USDT/USDC settle in minutes, no chargebacks, no card processor in our stack. For Reseller deals at $4,800+, net-30 wire is available. |
| &ldquo;What if you publish a leaked document I&rsquo;m subject to?&rdquo; | The methodology page documents our leak-provenance policy. We do not accept leaks without a verifiable chain of custody. If a subject of a leak emails the desk before publication, they can submit a comment we cite alongside the leak. |
| &ldquo;How do I know your dedupe is real?&rdquo; | Every issue publishes its dedupe ratio (e.g. 17:1) and the source list. The first three weeks of a subscription, an analyst gets a tracked log of which inputs landed in the issue and which were discarded. |
| &ldquo;Can I just hire an editorial freelancer for $1,500/mo?&rdquo; | You can. You will end up writing the dedupe rules and source list yourself and replacing the freelancer twice in eighteen months. Bureau is the source list and the rules, plus the writer. |

## Refund / cancellation

- Single + Bundle: month-to-month, cancel at end of period, full refund of first month if the dossier doesn&rsquo;t match the sample within 14 days.
- Reseller-license: setup fee refundable for 30 days after the first co-branded issue. Monthly fees cancel at end of period; no annual lock-in.

## Pricing power posture

Bureau prices to position. We are deliberately not the cheapest editorial product, and we are deliberately not the most expensive. The reference point is &ldquo;a fraction of a junior analyst&rsquo;s monthly cost&rdquo; — that anchors the value at every tier.
