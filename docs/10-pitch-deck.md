## 1 — Title

**BUREAU.**
Footnoted intelligence. Reseller-ready.
*A research syndicate.*

## 2 — The problem

Boutique vertical analysts spend Thursday night crawling regulator gazettes. Bloomberg is for traders. Substack is unsourceable. Google Alerts is duplicate-spam. The result: a senior partner re-keys footnotes from blog posts at 06:00 on Friday morning.

## 3 — The product

A weekly dossier per regulated vertical. Each claim mapped to a primary source via a dossier-ID like `§ FED-25-1144`. Five verticals at launch: fintech regulations · biotech grants · govtech procurement · climate-tech disclosures · semiconductor export controls. PDF + JSON/CSV feed + email.

## 4 — Why now

Three converging shifts in 2026:
- AI-summarizers have flooded the market with un-cited &ldquo;intelligence&rdquo; — driving demand for verifiable editorial.
- Regulator volume has roughly doubled since 2023 — the manual-crawl-on-Thursday-night problem is worse than ever.
- Boutique consultancies have re-staffed analyst desks (post-2024 layoffs at large vendors) and are looking for an editorial back-end.

## 5 — The reseller move

Same body. Different mastheads. We re-render every issue with the reseller&rsquo;s wordmark, accent, logo, and footer line. The body stays byte-identical. A vertical-SaaS partnerships team gets editorial-grade content under their brand without writing it. We get distribution.

## 6 — Business model

| Tier | Price | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Single | $499 / vertical / mo | Boutique consultancies, individual analysts |
| Bundle | $1,499 / mo (5 verticals) | Mid-size consultancies covering 2-3 verticals |
| Reseller-license | $4,800 setup + $1,200 / added vertical / mo | Vertical-SaaS partnerships, syndicators |

Crypto-only checkout (USDT/USDC). Net-30 wire for reseller deals.

## 7 — Methodology

Sourcing → dedupe → footnote → ship. Each issue publishes its dedupe ratio (typically 12:1 to 20:1 — we read 200-400, you read 20). All sources public. All claims footnoted. Leak-provenance policy documented. No retractions in 2026 W12-W19 (active period).
